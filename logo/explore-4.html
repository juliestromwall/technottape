Making TECH digital

Six treatments, all inside the lockup you already have: TECH digital · NOT solid · TAPE hollow. Shown on your background, on light, and at roughly the size it would sit in your site header.

Exactly what you pictured: the letters of TECH filled with 1s and 0s. Cute, and instantly readable as digital.
